FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can Immigro guarantee visa approval?+

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority. Immigro provides guidance and application/document support.

What documents will I need?+

Requirements vary by destination and visa type. Common documents include passport, ID/residence visa, CV, educational documents and experience certificates where applicable.
